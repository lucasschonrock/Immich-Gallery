# App Store Description

Bring your Immich photo library to the biggest screen in your home.

Immich Gallery is an Apple TV app that lets you browse, search, and enjoy your entire Immich library from the comfort of your couch. Relive family memories, share vacation photos, or turn your television into an ambient display during gatherings.

Beautiful full-screen slideshow mode with customizable playback options makes it easy to sit back and enjoy your photos exactly the way you want.

## Features

- Browse your entire photo library with smooth, fast infinite scrolling.
- View personal and shared albums.
- Explore photos by people, tags, external folders, and locations.
- Search and filter your library using Immich's search capabilities.
- Enjoy beautiful full-screen slideshow mode with customizable playback options, timing, transitions, and optional overlays.
- Connect to multiple Immich servers or accounts.
- Sign in with a password or an Immich API key.
- Bring favorite memories to the Apple TV Top Shelf for quick access.

## Private by Design

Your photos stay on your own Immich server. Immich Gallery connects directly to your existing Immich instance and does not upload, store, or process your photo library on third-party servers. The app includes zero telemetry.

Immich Gallery is an independent client for Immich and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Immich project.
